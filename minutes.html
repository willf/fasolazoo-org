Kalamazoo Singing

Kalamazoo Nature Center

Kalamazoo, Michigan

July 19, 2026

The 28th session of the Kalamazoo Annual Singing met at the Kalamazoo Nature Center on July 19, 2026. The class was called to order by Bess Fitzgerald leading 171. John Fink opened in prayer, and led 38b. Martha Beverly further welcomed singers, and led 283. Leaders: Becca Harrington 64; Samuel Sommers 544; Gillian Inksetter 516; Marjorie Harrington 275t; Jan May 469; Cecelia Kramer 157; Bridge Hill Kennedy 70b; Shannon Tibbs and Martha Beverly 86; Mark Clague, a professor at the University of Michigan, introduced his summer class, and invited 11 of his students to lead with assistance. Student Linden de Leeuw and Samuel Sommers 47b; Maria Schoon and Bess Fitzgerald 47t.

RECESS

Bess Fitzgerald and Beth Todd led 32t to bring the class together. Student leaders from the University of Michigan continued: Victoria Denne and Bridge Hill Kennedy 77b; Katrina O'Higgins and Becca Harrington 45t; Marissa Staffero and Gillian Inksetter 46; Thomas Odenheimer and John Fink 479; Emily Liang and Maygan Barker 49t; Claudia Gruett-Labus and Deborah Taube 186; Alex Pickell and Thomas Koehnline 39t; Montana Crawford with Samantha Adioetomo and Mary Adams 39b. Leaders continued: Samantha Adioetomo 384; Rob Tibbs 117; Anne Evers 290; Adrian Eldridge 508 in memory of Josie Hyde; Molly Williams 155; Thomas Koehnline 227; June Murphy 476.

RECESS

Carolyn Deacy brought the class back with 72b. Leaders: Orion Saiph and Gillian Inksetter 139; Mary Adams, Will Adams, and Thomas Koehnline 282; Jamie Hopkins 566; Laurel Cornell 503; Will Fitzgerald and Bess Fitzgerald with the O'Dell Family: Shannon O'Dell, Spencer O'Dell, Leonard O'Dell, Beckett O'Dell, and Merrick O'Dell. Deborah Taube 78; Bill McNiece 265; Will Grade 255; Ray Rechenberg 564; Ginny Landgraf 461; Brandon Buxton 161; Beth Todd 556; Doug Stapleton 440; Anne Missavage 276; Peter Trumbore 480; Lois Kiser 376; Mark Bowman 258; Midge Harder 532; Jim Helke 525.

Beth Todd said grace for the meal.

LUNCH

The afternoon session began with Marjorie Harrington leading 40. Leaders: Brad Baughman and Will Fitzgerald 385b; Daryl Chesney 492; Erin Campbell-Wagner 159; Barbara Lehr and Gillian Inksetter 426b; Tom Evers 448b; Alice Bean 285t; Steve Hall and Beth Todd 146. Becca Harrington led 335 as we took a collection. Further leaders: Carolyn Deacy 212; Marian Mitchell 528; Matthew Lind 145b; Terry Cunningham 192; Maygan Barker and Will Fitzgerald with Caleb Alman 213t; Bess Fitzgerald and Will Fitzgerald with the Pfund family: John Pfund, Katie Pfund, Jack Pfund, Zeke Pfund and Charlotte Pfund. Linda Heemskerk 107; Lori Freye 504; Lori Graber 136; Kathryn Buxton 51; Nancy Spates 567 with prayers for those fighting wildfires.

Reports: expenses were met; 79 different singers led 71 songs. They represented 11 U.S. states and one Canadian province. Thanks were extended to the officers, kitchen staff, and the staff at the Kalamazoo Nature Center. Announcements were made. Bess Fitzgerald and Maygan Barker led 62 as the closing song. John Fink offered the closing prayer, and the class was dismissed.

Chairman--Bess Fitzgerald; Chaplain--John Fink; Finance Committee--Becca Harrington; Secretary--Marjorie Harrington; Arranging Committee--Will Fitzgerald.
