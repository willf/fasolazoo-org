Arrival & Accessibility

General Entry: Enter at the front gate and drive slowly to the main parking lot. The walk into the Nature Center involves stairs and a long ramp.

Accessibility Drop-off:

To drive close to the building and drop off food or people who need accessibility, continue through the parking lot and turn right down a ramp to the lower level. An entry door is to the right.

Inside on the left is an elevator to the floor above where the singing and food will go. Restrooms are available on both levels.

Dinner on the Grounds

Local and regional singers are encouraged to support the mid-day meal with prepared food to share.

Health Notice

Please do not attend if you have any symptoms of illness before or on this day.
